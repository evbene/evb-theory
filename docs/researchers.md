Look for measurable timing patterns during:

- phase transitions  
- collapse events  
- decoherence  
- symmetry breaking  
- reinforcement cycles  

These signatures should appear across quantum, condensed‑matter, and macroscopic systems.

### **2. Boundary‑Driven Behavior**
Investigate how boundaries influence:

- coherence  
- collapse

- anomalous behavior  

Boundary‑timing is the central mechanism of EVB Theory.

### **3. Cross‑Scale Coherence**
Test whether coherence propagates across layers:

- quantum → atomic  
- atomic → molecular  
- molecular → condensed matter  
- condensed matter → macroscopic  

This is a key prediction of Version 6.

---

Evaluate EVB explanations for:

- second sound  
- negative group‑velocity phonons  
- acoustic black holes  
- BEC sound modes  
- plasma sound anomalies  
- metamaterial acoustics  

These domains provide clear timing‑cycle signatures.

---

### **5. Vacuum Structure**
Explore timing‑reinforcement behavior in:

- vacuum fluctuations  
- collapse‑cycle transitions  
- boundary‑driven vacuum effects  

EVB Theory predicts measurable structure even in apparent emptiness.
