# EVB Theory Overview  
### The Cross‑Scale Architecture

EVB Theory is built on four structural pillars:

1. **Boundary‑Timing**  
   Physical systems maintain coherence through timing relationships at their boundaries.

2. **Collapse‑Cycle Mechanics**  
   Every system transitions between reinforced and unreinforced states, producing measurable behavior.

3. **Nested Structural Hierarchy**  
   Systems organize into layers, each with its own timing rules and coherence conditions.

4. **Reinforced Coherence**  
   Stability arises when timing, structure, and interaction cycles align.
