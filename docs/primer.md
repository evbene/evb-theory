# EVB Primer  
### The Conceptual Foundation

The EVB Primer introduces the core ideas behind the theory in a simple, intuitive way.  
It explains *why* EVB Theory exists and *how* its structure unifies physical behavior across all scales.

---

## The Central Idea

Physical systems maintain stability through **boundary‑timing** — the timing relationships at their edges.  
These timing relationships determine:

- coherence  
- collapse  
- reinforcement  
- stability  
- transitions  
- anomalies  

Everything in EVB Theory flows from this single mechanism.

---

## Collapse‑Cycle Basics

Every system moves through repeating cycles:

- **Reinforced state** — stable, coherent  
- **Unreinforced state** — transitional, collapsible  

These cycles explain:

- quantum collapse  
- decoherence  
- sudden symmetry breaking  
- phase transitions  
- cross‑scale coherence behavior  

---

## Why Boundaries Matter

Boundaries are where timing rules are enforced.  
They determine:

- how systems hold together  
- how they lose coherence  
- how they interact  
- how they transition  
- how they reinforce stability  

The Primer shows how this applies from quantum scales all the way to cosmology.
